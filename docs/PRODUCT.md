# Lyra

Lyra is a desktop app for macOS and Windows that shows synchronized lyrics for whatever you're playing on Spotify — automatically, in real time, with no manual searching required.

---

## What It Does

As soon as you start playing a song in Spotify, Lyra finds the lyrics and highlights the current line as the music plays. When the song changes, the lyrics change. You never have to do anything.

Beyond just displaying lyrics, Lyra highlights individual words inside each line as they're sung — giving you a true karaoke-style wipe effect, not just a line jumping around.

You can also click any lyric line to jump directly to that point in the song.

---

## Display Modes

Lyra offers three ways to see your lyrics, depending on how you like to work or listen:

### Lyra Window
A full-featured window showing album artwork, playback controls (play/pause, skip, seek), and the complete lyric sheet — scrolling automatically to keep the current line centered. The background glows with colors drawn from the album art.

### Marquee
A dynamic panel: a compact, always-on-top overlay that floats above all your other windows. It shows the current lyric line and the next one coming up, with animated audio bars while music plays. It starts at the bottom center of the screen and you can drag it anywhere, lock it in place, and optionally show a spinning album cover. Let go of it and it stays clear of the menu bar, the notch, the Dock, the taskbar and the Karaoke lyrics, lining up with an edge or the middle of the screen when it is close to one. Push it against the top edge instead and it docks there, over the menu bar; under a MacBook notch, its lyrics sit below the notch. It hides while nothing is playing. It sizes itself to suit each display, or you can choose a size.

### Karaoke Overlay
A fullscreen transparent overlay — designed for use while watching something or doing anything else on your screen. It displays a small band of lyrics over whatever is behind it, with word-by-word highlighting and customizable position, alignment, size, and opacity.

---

## Settings and Customization

Each display mode has its own settings. You can control:

- Font size for each surface independently
- Which surfaces are visible
- Whether the Marquee shows album art, and whether it spins
- Karaoke position (top, center, bottom), text alignment, width, and background opacity
- Whether to hide all surfaces when music is paused
- Which monitor to use on multi-monitor setups
- Whether Lyra launches automatically at login

---

## When Lyrics Aren't Available

If no synchronized lyrics exist for a track, Lyra says so clearly rather than showing nothing or hanging. You can retry manually if you think it was a network hiccup.

---

## Platforms

Lyra runs on macOS and Windows.
